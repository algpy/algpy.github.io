01

Research with an
open-world view.

My work sits where representation learning meets the problem of not knowing every class in advance.

Modern visual models are often trained as if the label set were complete. I work on methods that make the unknown explicit: transferring structure from known classes, recognizing new categories, and learning online as samples arrive.

02

Selected work

A short record of research projects that shaped how I think about discovery, transfer, and uncertainty.

  1. 01

    ICCV 2023 · co-first author · Oct 2022 – Mar 2023

    Class-relation Knowledge Distillation for Novel Class Discovery

    Proposed a knowledge-distillation strategy that uses known-class data to support learning over unknown classes, achieving state-of-the-art results on nearly all evaluated datasets at the time.

    Paper ↗
  2. 02

    OOD-CV SSB · Generalized Category Discovery track · Jul – Sep 2023

    Adaptive Knowledge Transfer for Generalized Category Discovery

    Moved knowledge transfer from logits to feature representations, extending the idea to generalized category discovery and winning the ImageNet-1k track.

    Competition result
  3. 03

    ECCV 2026 · first author · Sep 2025 – Mar 2026

    DP-BOA: Dirichlet-Process Birth-or-Assign for On-the-Fly Category Discovery

    A Bayesian method for online category discovery that supports streaming sample classification while deciding when a new class should be born.

    Preprint ↗
