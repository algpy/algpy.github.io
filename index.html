01 / Research

Learning beyond a fixed label set.

My research asks how visual systems can transfer structure from known classes, recognize unfamiliar categories, and continue learning as data arrives.

Most visual models assume that every relevant class is known during training. I study methods that make the unknown explicit, combining representation learning, knowledge transfer, and Bayesian reasoning to build more adaptable systems.

Research areas

  • Machine learning
  • Computer vision
  • Weakly and unsupervised learning
  • Multimodal learning
  • Novel and generalized category discovery
  • Open-world and online learning

02 / Publications

Selected research

Two projects on transferring knowledge to unknown classes and discovering new categories online.

  1. 2023

    ICCV 2023 · Co-first author

    Class-relation Knowledge Distillation for Novel Class Discovery

    This work introduces a knowledge-distillation strategy that transfers class relationships from known categories to novel ones. The method uses known-class data to improve discovery of unseen classes and achieved state-of-the-art results on nearly all evaluated datasets at the time.

    Read the paper (DOI) ->

  2. 2026

    ECCV 2026 · First author · Accepted

    DP-BOA: Dirichlet-Process Birth-or-Assign for On-the-Fly Category Discovery

    DP-BOA is a Bayesian approach to online category discovery. It classifies streaming samples while deciding whether each sample belongs to an existing category or should trigger the birth of a new one.
